1. 3B — SHIELD TRIGGER AT CAMP REACH   [do first, on v81]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule (GameRules.md): wolves attack at range 3, elk and golems at 4, monoliths
at 5.
Bug (confirmed): use_support_abilities() (bot.cpp:1880) counts a camp as
pressure only within 3 tiles. A shield-holder tanking an elk or golem from 4
never shields.
Switch: Cfg::RANGE_PRESSURE.
    if (c.is_awake() && !c.is_dead() && cheb(c.position(), pos) <= (Cfg::RANGE_PRESSURE ? spec_of(c).reach : 3))
Only the shield trigger changes; burst_at, FIT_FIGHT and escape are left
alone. That is the difference from CAMP_GANK_GUARD, which failed against
zhunt because it made champions flee earlier.
Who is affected:
  * today: Varg (slot-5 pick on all 42 five-champion maps; shield 2.5 x Focus)
  * after 2C/2D: Heart
Gates:
  * G1 = the five-champion maps that have elk/golem groups, x zmeta/zgank/zmarc
    (an economy and survival change).
  * Then G2/G3 as usual.
  * Report camp-caused deaths (death context != champion) and gold@300.
Expected: small and positive, or nothing. It is cheap, so it is a good first
run of the new gate set.


2. 3A — SHIELD/HEAL AFTER AN ESCAPE STEP (narrowed from ESCAPE_SUPPORT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why ESCAPE_SUPPORT likely failed: its walk-compatible stun on the chaser took
the turn's single cast, and the cast went BEFORE escape(), which can block a
dash.
Refined: no stun, and cast only AFTER escape() has moved:
    if (escape()) {
        if (Cfg::ESCAPE_SHIELD) use_support_abilities();   // no-op if a dash used the cast
        return;
    }
at the lethal/losing escape in play_turn (bot.cpp:2415; the one at 2430 is
inside ISO_STEP, which is off), AND in
retreat_if_hurt()'s hunted branch (bot.cpp:1969). That branch is where most
low-HP champions flee, and the original plan missed it.
Casting after the step loses nothing against casting before it: enemies act
after our turn, so the shield or heal absorbs the same hits. And it can never
take a dash away from escape().
Who is affected:
  * Varg (shield), Willow (heal; gone if 2A ships), Heart (shield, once
    drafted)
  * Vesper, Tavi, Cassi and Issac have no shield/heal: no change for them.
Gates:
  * G1 = the 14 fight maps x zmarc/zgank/zhunt.
  * Then G2/G3.
  * Re-run once Heart is in the draft.
Prior: low (ESCAPE_SUPPORT +8 -13). Keep it only if it is level or better.


3. 3C — PAIRING FOR MELEE CHAMPIONS: ONLY AS A DRAFT-STEP KNOB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The original rule (pair every melee champion on fight maps, map_mode > 0) is
close to BUDDY (+7 -24, negative against every opponent). The all-champion
pairing in BRUISER_PRESET did not help either.
GEM_MAP_GUARD's +19 -11 on the adgato maps cannot be credited to pairing: it
shipped together with OWN_HALF's x1.3.
Narrowed use:
  V2 VEND_PAIR (Step 2A knob)
      Vendetta, who replaces a ranged body PAIRING used to guard, gets the
      lone-group penalty (group_score, bot.cpp:691) while invaders_recent().
  H/M variants
      The same for Heart / Marcella, only if the G2 death breakdown of their
      step shows isolated pick-offs of that champion.
Never on race maps (RACE_SOLO measured +3 -0 for removing pairing there).


4. 3D — set_priority(): PARKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* C8 (fighters first / fighters last) was neutral to worse.
* A priority set this round only takes effect next round, so the conditions
  in the original sketch (carrier near enemies, low HP) act one round late.
* Turns alternate team by team (A1 B1 A2 B2 ...; the starting team swaps each
  round). Priority only reorders our own bodies within that sequence.
If it is revisited, test one rule only: a carrier with >= 20 gems and an enemy
in sight takes the top priority. Gates G1 = 14 fight maps, then G2/G3.


5. 3E (NEW) — REFIT THE FIGHT MODEL FOR A BRUISER DRAFT   [after 2C or 2D]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIT_FIGHT (common.h, the 12-feature logistic "P(we die within 8 rounds)" at
threshold 0.20) was fitted on v30's ranged-draft skirmishes against
zgank/zdive0/zpoke. With 2-3 melee bruisers its "outmatched" call may be
wrong in both directions: they flee fights they would win, or stay in fights
they would lose. Research.md section 8 lists "a real fight-outcome model" as
the main untried structural idea.
Plan:
  1. Log the same 12 features plus the outcome from v82 games (with the
     accepted draft steps on) against the G2 bruiser suite.
  2. Refit; compare test AUC with the old weights on the same rows.
  3. A/B the new weights as Cfg::FIT_V2 at threshold 0.20. Do NOT sweep
     thresholds: 0.025, 0.043 and 0.35 all failed before.
Gates: G1 = 14 fight maps x zmarc/zgank/zhunt/zfish, then G2/G3/G4.
Train on DEV-half maps only, so the HOLD half stays clean (phase 0 section 4).
