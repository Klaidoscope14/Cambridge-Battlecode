1. HOW v81 DRAFTS TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The original plan's walk-through of `opening--` is correct.
`opening` is local, reset to 3 on every pick, and 2 on 3-champion maps
(DRAFT3 = 2). Each loop iteration decrements it before the "already picked"
test.

  Map size   v81 draft                        v65 ladder win %
  3 champs   C&I > Vesper > Tavi              45% (62 games)
  4 champs   C&I > Vesper > Tavi > Willow     59% (104)
  5 champs   ... > Willow > Varg              50% (246)
             (Varg on 70/70 maps by score: 8.2 + 2*cluster + 0.6 Tavi
             synergy, against Vendetta 9.0-9.4)

Map lists (most common draft size in 2,500 top-10 games):
  3 champs (12): 00anvil 00breadcrumbs 00collision 00fragment 00packs
                 01broken_curve 01gold_rush 01the_farm 02arena 02the_pack
                 02the_pond adgato_gemcombat1
  4 champs (16): 00gemfight 00interlock 00oasis 00quarry 00reverse_spiral 00rows
                 00rushbait_v2 00swirl 00the_line 00vault 01hooks 01shortcut
                 02guardians 02stalactites adgato_gemcombat2 adgato_gemcombat3
  5 champs (42): all other pool maps

IMPORTANT: draft order is also turn order. Within a team, higher priority acts
first, then draft order. Reordering the same champions therefore changes games
(slightly). A step that is meant to touch one map size must leave the pick
order of the other sizes untouched. That makes those games identical, a free
identity check.


2. WHAT THE ORIGINAL STEPS WOULD ACTUALLY DO (simulated)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Original 2A, opener list {Vesper, Tavi} (drop Willow):
    slot 4 = Varg on 68/70 maps (not Vendetta);
    slot 5 = Stellar on 41 maps, Vendetta on 18, Ryth on 9.
  So "drop Willow" mostly ADDS STELLAR. The new champion is chosen by
  score() with no plan behind it.
* Original 2B, opener list {Vesper, Vendetta, Tavi}: also changes 4-champion
  maps (Varg -> Vendetta) and the pick order on 5-champion maps. It is not a
  3-champion-only change.
* Original 2C, Marcella score 9.5: Varg scores 8.8 + 2*cluster, so Varg still
  wins on maps with cluster > 0.35. A score tweak does not force the slot.
* Original 2D: Heart of Ruin has no case in score() (it falls to the default,
  0), so score() never picks it.
Every refined step below forces its champion explicitly and keeps the other
slots and the pick order fixed.


3. STEP 2A — WILLOW -> VENDETTA (4- and 5-champion maps)   [do first]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Switch: Cfg::S2A_VENDETTA. The opener list becomes {Vesper, Tavi, Vendetta}.
  3 champs: unchanged (C&I, Vesper, Tavi). 12 maps, identical games.
  4 champs: C&I, Vesper, Tavi, Vendetta.
  5 champs: C&I, Vesper, Tavi, Vendetta, Varg (score() still picks Varg on
            70/70 maps).
Exactly one champion changes, at the same position.

Why first:
  * No new champion code; v81 already plays Vendetta.
  * Closest precedent: MELEE_OPENER Vendetta-for-Willow on v36all won +35 -27,
    but gold@300 fell by 70.
  * Willow earns the least of our bodies (469 gold a game); top-10 Vendetta
    earns 1,200.
  * Vendetta is in 80% of top-10 drafts.
Risks:
  * We lose our only healer and a ranged body that PAIRING keeps next to an
    ally; Vendetta is melee and ignores PAIRING.
  * MARCELLA_OPENER (Marcella in the same slot) failed: +61 -108, race
    +2 -17. That was a different champion, but the same slot.
Gates:
  * G1 on the 58 four- and five-champion maps.
  * G2 and G3 on all maps (the 3-champion games must be identical).
  * Report: gold@300, clears, and deaths of Vendetta vs the Willow she
    replaced.
Pre-registered tuning knobs (phase 1 section 4): V1 VEND_BUILD, V2 VEND_PAIR.
At most these two, DEV half then HOLD half (phase 0 section 4).


4. STEP 2B — 3-CHAMPION MAPS: C&I, VESPER, VENDETTA   [can run with 2A]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Switch: Cfg::S2B_THREE_VENDETTA. Only when bc::draft::team_size() <= 3, the
opener list is {Vesper, Vendetta}.
  3 champs: C&I, Vesper, Vendetta (Tavi out).
  4/5 champs: untouched (identical games).
Why:
  * Our weakest size (45%).
  * Every common top-10 3-pick has a melee bruiser: Heart/Vendetta/Vesper 53%
    (118), Heart/Marcella/Vesper 51% (95), Marcella/Tavi/Vendetta 55% (58).
  * Ours is Issac plus three ranged bodies.
Gates:
  * G1 = 12 three-champion maps x 2 sides x bruiser suite (120 games), then
    all 12 + zfish opponents (312).
  * G3 is identical on the other 58 maps by construction, so only the
    3-champion games carry signal.
Ladder caveat: only ~15% of ranked games are 3-champion maps. 100 ranked games
give ~15 games of evidence for 2B; ship it bundled with 2A and read its effect
from the size split in ladder_compare.
2A and 2B touch disjoint maps, so their effects simply add. Test them in one
batch and combine.

8. STOP RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* A step that is not level after its pre-registered knobs is dropped. The
  draft stays where it is.
* 2D needs 2C-2 (Heart) to have been at least level.
* After each accepted step, the user uploads (G4). Ranked play over 100+
  games, split by map size, decides whether the next step starts from it.
  A local pass with a ladder drop means: roll back, and record the step in
  Research.md section 6.


9. RESULTS (2026-10-30) — 2A REJECTED, 2B OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tests ran on AWS against v82base (v81 + 3B).

Step 2A (Willow -> Vendetta):
  * bruiser suite +66 -105
  * full pool rejected early by the SPRT at 1,152 games, +62 -120
  * deaths +2.7 a game, gold@300 -88
  * worse against the farming bots too (zrush +0 -7, zmono2 +4 -10)
Step 2B (3-champion maps): SOFT REJECT, KEPT OPEN FOR LATER
  * 3-champion maps +23 -38 (net -15, bound -15.6)
  * bruiser suite +15 -23
  * full pool +14 -25 (net -11, bound -12.5): passes
  * deaths 11.0 vs 8.0 on those maps
  It fails level-or-better but is not clearly worse. Willow is not drafted on
  3-champion maps, so the heal explanation below covers 2A only. Before a
  retry, run a per-champion breakdown of the 3-champion games.

Why: per champion, Vendetta out-earns Willow (1,032 vs 546 gold), but every
ally dies more and earns less:
  * Cassi & Issac 2.99 -> 3.63 deaths
  * Varg 1.55 -> 2.08 deaths; gold 1,305 -> 1,084
Willow heals 11,213 HP a game, about eleven full lives. She is our sustain,
and her low gold made her look like the easiest slot to give up. The tuning
knobs (Vendetta build, pairing) cannot replace the lost healing, so they were
not run.

Revised rule: KEEP WILLOW. The next Vendetta test should take Varg's slot on
5-champion maps: a like-for-like melee swap that isolates the champion.
The alternative is Tavi's slot.

13. STEP 2D (Tavi -> Heart) RESULTS (2026-10-30): FAILED, ROOT CAUSE FOUND,
    PARKED PENDING A BEHAVIOURAL FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Results:
  * 5-champion maps: bruiser +52 -79, full +72 -105
  * 4-champion maps: bruiser +33 -54, full +50 -79

Diagnosis (per champion):
  * Heart deals ~30% less camp damage and ~35% less champion damage than the
    Tavi it replaces.
  * It takes 3x the camp damage and 2.3x the champion damage; it dies 2.0 vs
    1.35 a game.
  * Allies also die more (Tavi's ranged damage and stun are gone).

Fix variants (pre-registered):
  h1  prep + Strength line                 bruiser +68 -102
  h2  plain Heart, Strength line           bruiser +65 -78  (best)
  h3  plain Heart, Focus/Health line       bruiser +77 -97
  All three are rejected early on the full pool. The fixes cut Heart's camp
  damage taken, but not its low output or the team's extra deaths.

Remaining cause: our engine plays Heart like a ranged farmer. Its value is the
dash-stun and the low-HP damage bonus in fights, which needs Heart-specific
fight logic: dive or peel with the dash-stun, and fight lower when allies are
near. That is the next fix to try before dropping Heart for good.


14. STEP 2D FIGHT-LOGIC FIXES (2026-10-30): ALL FAILED -> 2D DROPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On h2 (plain Heart in Tavi's slot), each vs v82base:
  hg    HEART_GUARD (dash-stun or shield a diver on an ally, then hold it in melee)
        bruiser +66 -88, full rejected early
  he    HEART_ESCORT (Heart farms next to an ally while invaders are about)
        bruiser +62 -96, full rejected early
  hc    HEART_CLUSTER (Heart prefers monster-free clusters x2)
        bruiser +65 -80, full rejected early
  hall  all three
        bruiser +75 -104, full rejected early
Against plain Heart (the logic alone), none passes DEV/HOLD.

A trace probe shows HEART_GUARD fires 30-178 turns a game and acts every time.
So the logic works as written; it does not turn into wins.

Two rounds of fixes (7 variants) failed, so step 2D is dropped:
  * Team damage to champions is about the same with Heart as with Tavi.
  * Heart itself dies 2.0-2.2 times a game against Tavi's 1.35, each death a
    kill and a 155-gem bag lost.
Pattern across all draft tests: replacing one of our RANGED bodies with a melee
one fails (Vendetta x3, Heart x2, the whole-draft swaps). Replacing melee with
melee (Varg -> Marcella) works.
Future draft tests should swap like for like, or come after the fight problem
the ladder shows (untraded pick-offs, Cassi & Issac dying most) is improved.
